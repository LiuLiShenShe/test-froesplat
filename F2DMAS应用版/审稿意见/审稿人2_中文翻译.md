# 审稿人2意见中文翻译

## 总体意见

Fang 等人的论文展示了一个很好的案例，即使用简单的消费级设备构建经济实惠的端到端自动化植物表型分析解决方案。本文的价值在于对所提出表型分析框架进行了极为详细的描述，并包含有见地的说明，尽管仍存在下列一些不足。我支持在解决以下问题后发表该论文。

## 具体意见

- 摘要：相关测量是否真的具有四位数字的精度？显然不是，见图 8。因此，R2 保留两位数字就足够了。
回复：
We thank the reviewer for pointing this out. We agree that reporting R² values with four decimal places may imply an unrealistic level of measurement precision for the phenotypic validation results. We have therefore revised the Abstract, the corresponding Results text, and Table 7 to report R² values with two decimal places. Specifically, the R² range in the Abstract has been changed from 0.8999–0.9879 to 0.90–0.99.
感谢审稿人指出这一点。我们同意，以四位小数报告 R² 值可能会使表型验证结果的测量精度显得不切实际。因此，我们已修改摘要、相应的结果文本以及表 7，将 R² 值报告为两位小数。具体而言，摘要中的 R² 值范围已从 0.8999–0.9879 更改为 0.90–0.99。

- 在缺少对应平均值的情况下，RMSE 的绝对值信息量有限；请提供相应平均值，或者改为呈现相对误差指标，例如百分比误差。
We thank the reviewer for this helpful suggestion. We agree that absolute RMSE values are difficult to interpret without a reference scale for each phenotypic trait. To address this concern, we revised the Abstract and the phenotypic measurement Results section to report the relative error metric MAPE together with RMSE. We also revised Table 7 by adding the mean manual measurement for each trait, so that the RMSE values can be interpreted against the corresponding trait scale. Specifically, the revised table now reports MAPE values of 6.91% for plant height, 4.50% for canopy width, 7.45% for leaf length, and 9.73% for leaf width. These changes make the measurement errors more interpretable while retaining RMSE as an absolute error metric in centimeters.
我们感谢审稿人提出的宝贵建议。我们同意，如果没有针对每个表型性状的参考尺度，绝对均方根误差（RMSE）值难以解释。为了解决这个问题，我们修改了摘要和表型测量结果部分，将相对误差指标平均绝对百分比误差（MAPE）与RMSE一起报告。我们还修改了表7，添加了每个性状的平均手动测量值，以便RMSE值可以根据相应的性状尺度进行解释。具体而言，修改后的表格现在报告的MAPE值分别为：株高6.91%，冠幅4.50%，叶长7.45%，叶宽9.73%。这些更改使测量误差更易于解释，同时保留了以厘米为单位的绝对误差指标RMSE。

- 第 2.1 节：该节第二段更适合放在引言中。
We thank the reviewer for this helpful suggestion. We agree that the second paragraph of Section 2.1 mainly provides background motivation and literature context rather than describing the system architecture itself. We have therefore moved this paragraph to the Introduction and revised it as a transition that motivates the need for an integrated workflow connecting low-cost image acquisition, preprocessing, 3D reconstruction, explicit meshing, scale recovery, and phenotypic measurement. Section 2.1 has been correspondingly streamlined so that it now focuses on the architecture and processing stages of F2DMAS.
感谢审稿人的宝贵建议。我们同意第 2.1 节第二段主要属于研究背景和文献动机，而不是系统架构本身的描述。因此，我们已将该段移至引言部分，并将其修改为引出集成式工作流程必要性的过渡段。相应地，我们也精简了第 2.1 节，使其更加聚焦于 F2DMAS 的系统架构和处理流程。


- 我很想知道，在典型固定场景或复杂场景下，单株植物采集原始数据通常需要多长时间。估算所提出流程的通量也会很有意思，例如单位时间内可处理多少株植物。
We thank the reviewer for this helpful suggestion. We have added the typical raw-data acquisition time and an estimated acquisition throughput to the Materials and Methods section. In our practical acquisition procedure, recording a complete multi-view video sequence for a single plant typically required approximately 1.5-2.0 min. This corresponds to an estimated manual acquisition throughput of about 30-40 plants per hour, excluding sample placement, scale-marker adjustment, and data transfer. We also clarified that the subsequent computational workflow is executed automatically after video upload, and that the reported training and mesh-extraction times describe the computational stage rather than the manual acquisition stage.
感谢审稿人的宝贵建议。我们已在“材料与方法”部分补充了典型原始数据采集时间和估算采集通量。在实际采集中，单株植物完整多视角视频序列通常需要约 1.5-2.0 min，对应的人工采集通量约为每小时 30-40 株，不包括样品摆放、尺度标记调整和数据传输时间。我们还明确说明，视频上传后的后续计算流程是自动执行的，文中已有训练时间和网格提取时间反映的是计算阶段耗时，而非人工采集阶段耗时。

- 希望作者能提供更多关于算法和模型的软件实现细节，例如所使用的框架、编程库等。此外，GIT 仓库中包含大量脚本，其中一些脚本名称并不总是自解释的。因此，建议加入说明，将所有步骤或操作映射到仓库中的具体软件模块或脚本。若能包含一些测试数据集，也会很有帮助。
We thank the reviewer for pointing out the need for reproducibility details. We have added the GPU configuration and training environment in the Materials and Methods section. The revised manuscript now states that all experiments were conducted on a Windows 10 workstation with 128 GB RAM and two NVIDIA RTX A6000 GPUs. We also report that the main workflow was implemented in Python 3.11 and that the GPU-accelerated modules were executed using PyTorch 2.0.0 in a CUDA-enabled environment.
Regarding test data, we have clarified the data availability statement. Because the raw video files are relatively large, representative sample inputs and intermediate outputs will be made available upon reasonable request or through the public repository after data organization is completed.
感谢审稿人指出可复现性细节的不足。我们已在“材料与方法”部分补充 GPU 配置和训练环境，包括 Windows 10 工作站、128 GB 内存、两块 NVIDIA RTX A6000 GPU、Python 3.11，以及基于 PyTorch 2.0.0 和 CUDA 环境执行的 GPU 加速模块。
关于测试数据，我们补充说明了代表性输入数据和中间结果的可获得方式。由于原始视频体积较大，代表性样例输入和中间输出将在数据整理完成后通过公开仓库提供，或可根据合理请求获取。

- 讨论部分完全缺少对文献的引用，这一点比较奇怪。通常而言，讨论部分用于将本研究获得的发现与已有研究积累进行比较，因此预期应引用前人工作。与此同时，方法部分包含大量引用。请考虑对这些章节进行重组。
We thank the reviewer for this important suggestion. We agree that the original Discussion did not sufficiently relate our findings to previous studies, while several literature-oriented statements were concentrated in the Methods section. To address this concern, we revised the Discussion by adding relevant citations and explicitly comparing our findings with recent studies on low-cost image-based plant phenotyping, vision foundation model-assisted plant segmentation, NeRF/SAM-based phenotyping, and Gaussian Splatting-based plant reconstruction.

Specifically, we added discussion of recent work such as image-based high-throughput plant phenotyping reviews, deep learning for 3D plant phenomics, IPENS, PlantGaussian, Cotton3DGaussians, and LCR-GS. These additions help clarify how F2DMAS differs from existing approaches: F2DMAS emphasizes an integrated smartphone-video workflow, foreground extraction before 2DGS optimization, and surface-oriented 2DGS plus TSDF meshing for measurable thin-leaf plant structures.

We also checked the Methods section to avoid unnecessary literature-style discussion there, while keeping citations that are needed to identify the algorithms and technical components used in the workflow.
我们感谢审稿人提出的这项重要建议。我们同意原讨论部分未能充分将我们的发现与之前的研究联系起来，而一些文献相关的论述则集中在方法部分。为了解决这个问题，我们修改了讨论部分，添加了相关的参考文献，并明确地将我们的发现与近期关于低成本基于图像的植物表型分析、基于视觉基础模型的植物分割、基于NeRF/SAM的表型分析以及基于高斯散射的植物重建的研究进行了比较。

具体而言，我们增加了对近期工作的讨论，例如基于图像的高通量植物表型分析综述、用于三维植物表型组学的深度学习、IPENS、PlantGaussian、Cotton3DGaussians和LCR-GS。这些新增内容有助于阐明F2DMAS与现有方法的区别：F2DMAS强调集成智能手机视频工作流程、在二维几何结构（2DGS）优化之前进行前景提取，以及采用面向表面的2DGS和TSDF网格划分来获取可测量的薄叶植物结构。

我们还检查了方法部分，以避免不必要的文献式讨论，同时保留识别工作流程中使用的算法和技术组件所需的引用。
