Base+SAM3 directly uses SAM3 masks without the additional FSAM3 post-processing steps, whereas Base+FSAM3 uses the complete FSAM3 module with SAM3-based mask generation and subsequent foreground refinement. Compared with Base+SAM3, Base+FSAM3 improves PSNR from 30.14 dB to 30.50 dB, increases SSIM from 0.9656 to 0.9687, reduces LPIPS from 0.0426 to 0.0397, and decreases training time and mesh extraction time from 7,826.4 s and 92.8 s to 6,541.3 s and 73.6 s, respectively. These comparisons indicate that the improvement is not simply caused by using SAM3 as the segmentation backbone, but also comes from the additional FSAM3 foreground refinement steps, which reduce incomplete masks, plant-background adhesion, noisy connected regions, and residual artifacts.

- 叶宽结果相对其他性状明显较弱，R2 约为 0.90，MAPE 为 9.73%。作者在讨论部分提到了这一点，但我建议稍微更深入地分析。这里有两个非致命问题。众所周知，叶宽测量非常困难。这种偏差是来自分割、2DGS 表面膨胀，还是 TSDF 网格化？如果将数据转换为点云格式，是否可以通过点云测量叶宽，从而避免由网格引入的误差？
回复：
We thank the reviewer for this insightful suggestion. We agree that leaf-width measurement is more challenging than the other traits and deserves a more explicit discussion. Following the reviewer’s comment, we expanded Section 4.5, “Limitations and Future Work”, to analyze the possible sources of the relatively weaker leaf-width performance.

In the revised discussion, we clarify that the leaf-width bias may arise from multiple stages, including small segmentation boundary shifts, local 2DGS surface expansion or depth instability under oblique views, and boundary smoothing or dilation during TSDF fusion and mesh extraction. We also added our diagnostic observation from a representative thin-leaf sample: when the reconstructed data were converted to a Gaussian point-cloud representation, the leaf-width measurement error was reduced compared with the TSDF-mesh-based result, suggesting that mesh extraction contributes to part of the error. However, the error was not completely eliminated in the point-cloud representation, indicating that meshing is not the only source of the bias.

We have therefore revised the Limitations and Future Work section to state that point-cloud-based measurement may reduce mesh-induced boundary error, but high-precision leaf-width extraction still requires further improvements in plant-aware Gaussian optimization, topology-aware pruning, and edge-aware meshing.
我们感谢审稿人提出这一有价值的建议。我们同意，与其他性状相比，叶宽测量更具挑战性，因此需要在稿件中进行更明确的讨论。根据审稿人的意见，我们扩展了 4.5 “Limitations and Future Work” 部分，对叶宽测量精度相对较弱的可能来源进行了分析。

在修订后的讨论中，我们说明叶宽偏差可能来自多个阶段，包括分割边界的轻微偏移、斜视角下 2DGS 局部表面膨胀或深度不稳定，以及 TSDF 融合和网格提取过程中产生的边界平滑或轻微膨胀。我们还加入了一个代表性薄叶样本上的诊断性观察：当重建结果转换为 Gaussian point-cloud 表示后，叶宽测量误差相较于 TSDF mesh 结果有所降低，说明网格提取确实贡献了部分误差。然而，点云表示下误差并未完全消除，表明叶宽偏差并非仅由网格化造成。

因此，我们修改了 Limitations and Future Work 部分，明确指出点云测量可能降低 mesh-induced boundary error，但高精度叶宽提取仍需要进一步改进 plant-aware Gaussian optimization、topology-aware pruning 和 edge-aware meshing。
